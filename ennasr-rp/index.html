A city that feels like home

Ennasr RP is built for Tunisian players. You roleplay in Darija, patrol streets that look familiar, and deal with the kind of situations you would actually run into.

Whether you want to chase suspects in a police cruiser or run a café on the corner, there is a place for you here.

Rules in short

The full list is pinned in our Discord. These are the ones that matter most.

  1. Stay in characterKeep out-of-game talk out of the roleplay.
  2. No random killingEvery fight needs a reason and a story behind it.
  3. Respect everyoneNo insults, hate or harassment. Staff decisions are final.
  4. Follow staffIf a staff member gives an instruction, follow it and appeal later.
